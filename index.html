05/ QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

What is CreatorOrbit?

CreatorOrbit is an independent digital studio that combines website development, e-commerce development, persuasive copywriting, SEO and answer engine optimization (AEO). The aim is simple: build websites that turn visitors into customers.

What services does CreatorOrbit offer?

CreatorOrbit offers custom website development, e-commerce development, landing pages, website and sales-page copywriting, product descriptions, brand messaging, technical and on-page SEO, content optimization, AEO strategy, and conversion-focused layouts and lead generation systems.

What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?

AEO is the practice of structuring a website's content so that search engines and AI assistants can understand it and use it directly as an answer. It combines clear writing, question-led content and structured data alongside traditional SEO.

Why combine a developer and a writer?

A website has to work technically and persuade people to act. Having development and copywriting in one studio means design, words and technology are planned together instead of being handed between separate teams.

Does CreatorOrbit build e-commerce websites?

Yes. CreatorOrbit builds custom e-commerce experiences, including ShopNiva, a store for a contemporary women's accessories brand covering product discovery, personalization and checkout.

How does a project with CreatorOrbit work?

Every project follows four stages: Discover (understand the business, audience and goals), Strategize (define direction, content structure and user journey), Build (design, develop and integrate the experience) and Deliver (test, refine and hand over).

How much does a website cost?

Cost depends on the scope, number of pages, features and content needed. Email creatororbit48@gmail.com with a short description of the project to discuss it.

How can I start a project?

Email creatororbit48@gmail.com or message @creator_orbit on Instagram with a short description of what you want to build.
